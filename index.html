QUANTUM QUEST

THE MEASUREMENT MYSTERY

A 3D first-person game that teaches quantum physics by letting players run real experiments on a qubit. By [Team Name], [Hackathon Name].

PLAY THE PROJECT

▶️ Launch the game

Runs in Chrome or Edge, no install or login. Needs an internet connection.

30-SECOND GUIDE

Click Enter Laboratory, walk with WASD, look with the mouse, and press E at the glowing console. Then try R prepare · H Hadamard · M measure · Z/X/C run 20/50/100 trials. Esc frees the mouse for clicking buttons.

WHAT YOU WILL SEE

1 · SuperpositionA qubit with amplitudes for 0 and 1
2 · MeasurementOne random outcome, state collapses
3 · ProbabilityLive meters for P(0) and P(1)
4 · Repeated trialsChart of experiment vs expected
5 · Hadamard gateH once = superposition, twice = back to 0

WHY IT IS ACCURATE

It runs a real single-qubit simulator with complex amplitudes, P(0)=|α|² and P(1)=|β|², true random sampling and state collapse. It does not claim that one measurement proves a distribution, and it does not present any interpretation of quantum mechanics as settled.

BUILT WITH

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js (WebGL) and the Web Audio API. No backend, no API keys.
